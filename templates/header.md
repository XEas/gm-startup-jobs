- 🎓 **Internships** and **New Grad** roles are in separate tables.

- 📧 **Email** means the startup takes applications by email. See [how to cold-email a startup](#-how-to-apply-to-email-only-startups) below.
- Roles that are closed or haven't been confirmed in 60 days go into the collapsed **Possibly stale / closed** section at the bottom.

## 📧 How to apply to email-only startups

Small startups often hire straight from the founder's inbox. A good cold email is short:

1. **Subject:** `[Role] application: [Your name], [school/program or one-line hook]`
2. **Line 1:** who you are and which role you want.
3. **Lines 2–3:** one or two concrete things you've built or done that fit what *they* build. Link them.
4. **Line 4:** availability (term, dates, location or remote).
5. Attach a one-page PDF resume. No cover letter needed.
6. Follow any **apply tips** listed under the link. They come straight from the startup.
7. If you hear nothing, send one polite follow-up after about a week. Then move on.
